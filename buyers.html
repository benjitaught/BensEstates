Step by Step

The Home Buying Process

1

Get Pre-Approved for a Mortgage

Before you start touring homes, connect with a lender to get pre-approved. This tells you exactly how much you can afford and makes your offers more competitive. I have trusted lender partners I can refer you to.

2

Define Your Wish List & Must-Haves

We'll sit down (or hop on a call) to talk through your needs, wants, and deal-breakers — bedrooms, location, school districts, commute, lifestyle. This helps me narrow the search and find homes that truly fit.

3

Tour Homes

I'll set up custom MLS alerts so you see new listings the moment they hit the market. We'll schedule tours, and I'll point out things — good and bad — that you might miss as a buyer.

4

Make an Offer

Found the one? I'll run a detailed market analysis to determine a competitive offer price, then craft a strong offer with terms designed to protect you and beat the competition.

5

Negotiate & Go Under Contract

I'll negotiate on your behalf to get the best price and terms. Once we reach an agreement, we'll be "under contract" — and the due diligence period begins.

6

Home Inspection & Due Diligence

We'll hire a licensed inspector to thoroughly evaluate the property. If issues arise, I'll help you negotiate repairs, credits, or a price reduction — protecting your investment.

7

Final Walkthrough & Closing

Just before closing, we'll do a final walkthrough to confirm everything is in order. Then we head to the closing table — sign the papers, get the keys, and celebrate your new home!
