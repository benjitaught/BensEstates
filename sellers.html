Step by Step

The Home Selling Process

1

Free Home Valuation & Strategy Session

We start with a Comparative Market Analysis to determine the optimal listing price — high enough to maximize your return, realistic enough to attract serious buyers quickly.

2

Prepare & Stage Your Home

I provide a detailed prep checklist and staging recommendations. Small investments in presentation consistently yield outsized returns. I can connect you with professional stagers if needed.

3

Professional Photography & Marketing Launch

Once your home is ready, we schedule professional photos, video, and 3D tours. Then we launch your listing with a coordinated marketing campaign across all channels simultaneously.

4

Showings, Open Houses & Feedback

I manage all showing requests and provide you with regular feedback from buyers. We'll track interest levels and adjust strategy if needed to keep momentum strong.

5

Receive & Negotiate Offers

When offers come in, I'll walk you through each one — price, contingencies, financing, and closing timeline. I'll negotiate firmly to get you the best possible outcome.

6

Under Contract & Due Diligence

Once we accept an offer, the buyer conducts inspections. I'll help you navigate repair requests and keep the deal on track through the due diligence period.

7

Closing Day

We'll coordinate with the title company, lender, and buyer's agent to ensure a smooth closing. You sign, collect your proceeds, and hand over the keys. Congratulations!
